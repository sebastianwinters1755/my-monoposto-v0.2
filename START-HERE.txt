RACE ENGINEER  -  TEST VERSION 0.2
===================================

What this is
------------
A READING TEST. It watches Monoposto on your tablet and shows what it can read
(positions, gaps, tyres, flags, penalties, pit stops, tyre wear...). It does not
talk and gives no advice yet. The goal is to find out how well it reads your game.

You do NOT need Android Studio, Git or any command line.
You only need your web browser (Chrome or Edge) and your GitHub account.


PART A - Put the project on GitHub (one time)
---------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the ZIP file you downloaded and choose "Extract All...", then "Extract".
   Open the extracted folder. Inside you will see a folder called "RaceEngineer".

2. Open the "RaceEngineer" folder. You should see these items:
       .github     app     build.gradle.kts     gradle.properties     settings.gradle.kts
   (".github" starts with a dot. That is normal. It must be there.)

3. In your browser go to  github.com  and sign in.

4. Top right, click the  +  button, then  "New repository".
      - Repository name:  race-engineer
      - Choose  "Private"
      - Do NOT tick any other boxes
   Click the green  "Create repository"  button.

5. On the next page, click the blue link  "uploading an existing file".

6. Go back to the "RaceEngineer" folder in File Explorer. Press Ctrl+A to select
   EVERYTHING inside it, then drag it all onto the browser page where it says
   "Drag files here to add them to your repository".
   Wait until the upload finishes. In the list you MUST see:
       .github/workflows/build.yml
       app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
       (and several .kt files under app/src/main/java/com/raceengineer/app/)
   If you do not see ".github/workflows/build.yml", use the backup method at the
   bottom of this file.

7. Scroll down and click the green  "Commit changes"  button.


PART B - Let GitHub build the app (5 to 10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------
1. Click the  "Actions"  tab at the top of your repository.
   If you see a green button "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them",
   click it.
2. You will see a run called "Build Race Engineer APK" with a spinning yellow dot.
   Wait until it turns into a GREEN TICK.
3. If it turns into a RED CROSS: click it, click "build" on the left, click the step
   that has the red mark, and take a screenshot. Send me that screenshot and I will
   fix it. This can happen on a first build and is normal.
4. When it is green: click the run (its name), scroll down to "Artifacts", and click
   "RaceEngineer-APK". A ZIP downloads. Extract it. Inside is  app-debug.apk .


PART C - Install on the tablet
------------------------------
1. Move app-debug.apk to the tablet (email it to yourself, Google Drive, or USB cable).
2. On the tablet open the file. If Android says it cannot install from this source,
   tap "Settings" and switch on "Allow from this source", go back and install.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap "More details", then "Install anyway".
   (It warns because the app is not from the Play Store.)


PART D - The test
-----------------
1. Open "Race Engineer" and tap  START.
2. First time only: it sends you to a settings page. Switch ON "Allow display over
   other apps" for Race Engineer, go back, and tap START again. Allow notifications.
3. Android asks to share your screen. Choose  "Entire screen"  and tap "Start now".
4. Open Monoposto and start a race.
5. A small box appears at the BOTTOM-LEFT of the screen showing what the app reads.
6. To keep a picture and its reading for checking:
      - pull down the notification shade and tap  "Save snapshot", or
      - in Race Engineer tap  "SNAPSHOT IN 8 s"  and switch to the game.
   Take snapshots in different situations: normal lap, start of a lap when the full
   table opens, yellow flag, penalty, in the pit lane, after a pit stop, and in both
   camera views.
7. When you come back to Race Engineer you can see the last reading from the game.
   Tap STOP when you are finished.


PART E - What to send me
------------------------
- The snapshots. Open the "My Files" app on the tablet:
      Images  >  RaceEngineer      (the pictures)
      Downloads  >  RaceEngineer   (the matching .txt readings)
  Send me 4 or 5 pairs (picture + text file with the same date and time).
- A screenshot of the Race Engineer screen after a race.
- Tell me anything odd: lag, heat, the game stuttering, the box in a strange place.


BACKUP METHOD if ".github" did not upload
-----------------------------------------
1. In your repository click  "Add file"  >  "Create new file".
2. In the name box type exactly:   .github/workflows/build.yml
   (typing the slashes creates the folders automatically)
3. Open the file  build.yml-backup.txt  (next to this file), copy everything, and paste
   it into the big text box on GitHub.
4. Click  "Commit changes"  (twice if asked) and continue with Part B.
